Privacy policy

Stream Highlight Pipeline uses Google authorization to manage approved YouTube uploads, read channel/video information and analytics, and store encrypted backups in Google Drive. Backup authorization is separate and limited to Drive files the client creates or opens.

Approved uploads go to YouTube; encrypted backups go to the authorized Google Drive account. Google account data is used for these features, not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated parties.

Stored records and backups remain until deleted or removed under configured retention settings. Revoking access stops future access but does not erase existing local records or backups; the owner must delete those separately.

You can revoke Google access. Google services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

This information site has no app login, tracking scripts or data collection forms. GitHub hosts it and may process ordinary connection logs under its privacy statement.

Public contact — do not post private data or credentials.
